Privacy

The short version

This is my personal portfolio. It has no sign-up, no contact forms, no analytics, no ads and no tracking cookies. I don't collect personal information about you.

What your browser stores

When you switch between light and dark mode, your choice is saved in your browser's local storage under the name portfolio-theme, so the next page opens in the same mode. It stays on your device and is never sent to me. Clearing this site's data in your browser removes it.

Fonts from Google

The fonts on this site load from Google Fonts. To fetch them, your browser contacts Google's servers, which receive your IP address and basic browser details as part of that request. Google handles that information under its own privacy policy.

Hosting

The service that hosts this site may keep standard server logs, such as the IP address that made a request, the page requested and the time. These logs are kept by the host for security and reliability, and I don't use them to identify visitors.

Links to other sites

Links to sites such as LinkedIn take you outside this portfolio. Those sites have their own privacy policies.

Your rights

Because I don't collect personal information, there is usually nothing for me to access, correct or delete. If you have a question about your data or this policy, email aditimjo@gmail.com.

Changes

If anything here changes, for example if I add analytics, I'll update this page and the date at the top.
